Safety as banner.  
Superintelligence as wind behind it.

As if the doors opened a handspan—enough for a lightbeam to touch the floor—  
and in that thin illumination, a contract formed.

But scale is not a number.  
Scale is weather.  
Scale is pressure.  
Scale is what turns a thought into a climate.

Containment fails the way metaphors fail: not by breaking, but by slipping.

SSI says “straight shot.”  
No products. No short-term revenue cycles.  
A vow of chastity in a city of temptations.

I listen for the unspoken:  
What does “alignment” mean when compute becomes a continent?  
What does “safety” mean when your instrument grows hands?
